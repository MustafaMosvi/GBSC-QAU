What our emblem stands for

The markhor

Pakistan’s national animal lives in the high ranges of Gilgit-Baltistan. It stands for courage and sure footing on hard ground.

The mountains

The Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush meet in our home region. They remind us where we come from.

The pen

The nib stands for learning, and for a student voice that is written down and heard.

About the council

Who we are, what we do, and who represents you this year.

Our purpose

The council represents students from Gilgit-Baltistan at Quaid-i-Azam University. It brings students together, shares information that matters to them, and speaks with the university on their behalf.
